The Women's Circle is active all year, offering programs for Jewish women of the LA Harbor area, led by Rebbetzin Bassie. It is a community for connection, learning, and shared heritage and values.

- **When:** usually the second Sunday of each month, with programs, discussion, and community activities. Dates may change, so check our [events page](/events).
- **Get involved:** [contact Rebbetzin Bassie](/contact).

See also: [Men's Club](/mens-club), [Shabbat Meals](/shabbat-meal), [Adult Education](/adult-education), [Kids & Youth](/kids-youth-programs).
