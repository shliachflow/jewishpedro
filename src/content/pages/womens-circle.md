## Jewish Women's Circle

Women gathering for learning and community throughout the year

## Welcome to Our Community

The Jewish Women's Circle of S. Pedro is active all year round with different activities and programs geared towards women, led by Rebbetzin Bassie. Join us for programs that bring women together to learn and celebrate our shared heritage and values.

### Get Connected

Reach out to Rebbetzin Bassie to get involved.

### Gatherings

We gather from time to time throughout the year for special programs, discussions, and community building activities.
