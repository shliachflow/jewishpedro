## Jewish Women's Circle

A vibrant community of women gathering for connection, learning, and meaningful experiences throughout the year

## Welcome to Our Community

The Jewish Women's Circle of S. Pedro is active all year round with different activities and programs geared towards women, led by Rebbetzin Bassie. Join us for inspiring programs, meaningful connections, and enriching experiences designed to bring women together in celebration of our shared heritage and values.

### Get Connected

Ready to join our vibrant community? Reach out to Rebbetzin Bassie to get involved and discover all the wonderful opportunities awaiting you.

[Contact Rebbetzin Bassie](/contact)

### Monthly Gatherings

We typically meet on the second Sunday of each month for special programs, discussions, and community building activities.
