Chabad of San Pedro is a Jewish community center affiliated with Chabad-Lubavitch, a global movement with thousands of centers worldwide. Our mission is to build a warm, welcoming Jewish community for people of every background and level of observance.

## Rabbi Yisroel Shuchat

Rabbi Yisroel is the founder, director, and spiritual leader of the center. He grew up in a family of Chabad emissaries and was ordained in Brooklyn. He has taught in several states, served at Jewish summer camps, and took part in Chabad's Roving Rabbis program.

## Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat

Bassie is the co-founder and program director. She was raised in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, and has worked as an educator. She is known for her cooking, her challah, and her talent for building relationships.

## Our story

Rabbi Yisroel and Bassie moved to San Pedro in the summer of 2021 to establish a Chabad center. They have two children, Aliza and Mendel. Since then the center has grown to offer Shabbat services, classes, holiday programs, fresh challah, and more.
