## Our Story

Welcome to Chabad of S. Pedro, California. We're here for every Jew in our area. We believe every Jew is part of our extended family, and we want our center to feel like a home away from home.

Chabad of S. Pedro is affiliated with Chabad Lubavitch, a global movement that shares Judaism with Jews of every background and affiliation. Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi founded it in the 18th century, and it continues to follow the teachings and guidance of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson, of righteous memory. Across the globe, Chabad Lubavitch operates thousands of centers, known as Chabad Houses, where Jews come together to learn, pray, and celebrate (click [here](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/36226/jewish/About-Chabad-Lubavitch.htm) to read all about Chabad).

In the summer of 2021, Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat moved to S. Pedro, California, intending to establish a Chabad center in the area. Their mission is to reach out to fellow Jews through holiday programs, freshly baked challah deliveries, and more.

Rabbi Yisroel and Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat, proud parents of Aliza and Mendel, invite you to join them at Chabad of S. Pedro. Come for Shabbat, a class, or a holiday, and meet the community. We'd love to see you.

Follow us on social media for the latest news, and feel free to [contact us](/contact) at any time.

## Meet the Team

![Rabbi Yisroel Shuchat](/images/about-rabbi-yisroel.png)

### Rabbi Yisroel Shuchat

**FOUNDER, DIRECTOR, AND SPIRITUAL LEADER**

Rabbi Yisroel Shuchat was born in Caracas, Venezuela, and grew up in a family of Chabad emissaries. He studied abroad at the Educational Institute and Talmudical Seminary Oholei Torah in Brooklyn, New York. He further pursued his rabbinical ordination at the Central Tomchei Temimim Lubavitch Yeshiva at the Chabad Headquarters in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. Rabbi Yisroel also spent time in Argentina, studying and assisting younger students. Additionally, he worked as a staff member at the Elementary and High School divisions in Yeshiva Schools of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He has taught in various schools in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and California. Rabbi Yisroel enjoys connecting with people, often over coffee, and loves nature, the outdoors, and writing. Over the years he has visited different Chabad Houses across the United States, assisted during holidays, and participated in the Roving Rabbis internship program. He has also served as a counselor and staff member at numerous United States and Canada camps.

![Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat](/images/about-rebbetzin-bassie.png)

### Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat

**CO-FOUNDER AND PROGRAM DIRECTOR**

Rebbetzin Bassie Shuchat was born and raised in the Crown Heights neighborhood of Brooklyn, New York. She received her education at the local Beth Rivkah school and a year abroad at the Bais Chana seminary in Safed, Israel. Upon returning to Brooklyn, she continued her studies at a seminary. After getting married, Rebbetzin Bassie worked as an educator in various roles within the local schools of Crown Heights. She loves building relationships and is known for connecting easily with people. She is a great cook, and you're welcome to taste her creations yourself. In her free time she enjoys reading, time with her family, and nature. Like Rabbi Yisroel, Rebbetzin Bassie has visited numerous Chabad Houses and has spent summers working in different camps in the United States and Canada.

## Our Mission

At Chabad S. Pedro Jewish Center, our mission is to create a vibrant Jewish community where everyone, regardless of background or level of observance, feels welcome, valued, and inspired. We aim to provide authentic Jewish experiences, education, and resources to enrich the lives of all who walk through our doors.

## About Chabad Lubavitch

Chabad-Lubavitch is a movement that teaches Jewish philosophy, the deepest dimension of G-d’s Torah, understanding and recognition of the Creator, the role and purpose of creation, and the importance and unique mission of each creature. It has a history of over 250 years. Guided by the teachings of the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson of righteous memory, it now comprises a global organization with thousands of emissaries and institutions serving the Jewish community. [Click here](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/36226/jewish/About-Chabad-Lubavitch.htm) to learn more.
