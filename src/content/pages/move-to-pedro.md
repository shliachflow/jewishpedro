San Pedro is a coastal neighborhood of Los Angeles, about 35 minutes from Pico-Robertson outside rush hour, and an affordable place for Jewish families to put down roots. Our center sits in the Vista del Oro neighborhood.

## Housing

The median home price is around $800,000 (2026), well below Pico-Robertson, Hancock Park, and Beverlywood. Median rent for a three-bedroom is about $3,800 a month, also lower than most comparable LA Jewish neighborhoods.

## Getting around

- Pico-Robertson: about 35–45 minutes
- Long Beach Chabad: 20–25 minutes
- Kaiser Permanente Harbor City: 13–15 minutes

Rush hour can add 15–30 minutes.

## Lifestyle

Beaches, trails on the Palos Verdes peninsula, and the West Harbor waterfront redevelopment, expected to open in 2026.

## Community

Ours is a small but growing community with weekly Shabbat services, Torah classes, holiday programs, and Shabbat meals hosted by the Rabbi and Rebbetzin. It's a community you can help build.

## What's not here yet

San Pedro has no Jewish day school, mikvah, eruv, or daily minyan yet. Families currently use Chabad of South Bay in Lomita for preschool and a shuttle to LA day schools, and Long Beach Chabad for daily prayer and mikvah.
