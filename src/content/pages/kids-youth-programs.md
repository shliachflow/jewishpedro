Chabad of San Pedro serves the Jewish community from infants to seniors. We do not currently run dedicated kids or youth programs, but we would love to start them based on family interest. Once enough families are ready, we'll launch.

## Programs we'd like to offer

- **Mommy & Me:** early childhood programs for parents and toddlers
- **Hebrew School:** Hebrew language, prayers, and Jewish heritage
- **CKids:** Jewish learning and activities for elementary-age children
- **CTeen:** programs for teens focused on Jewish identity, community service, and leadership
