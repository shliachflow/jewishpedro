## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Youth & Family Programs

We are here to serve the Jewish community—from infants to seniors and everyone in between!

🌟 Community-Driven Programming: While we don't currently offer dedicated kids and youth programs, we'd love to make them happen based on your family's interests and needs.

If your children are interested in participating, let us know! Whether it's Mommy & Me, Hebrew School, CKids, CTeen, or any other program, we're ready to bring it to life when we have families ready to join.

### 👶 Mommy & Me

Interactive early childhood programs for parents and toddlers to explore Jewish traditions together.

### 📚 Hebrew School

Comprehensive Jewish education program teaching Hebrew language, prayers, and Jewish heritage.

### 🎉 CKids

Fun and engaging programs for elementary school children featuring Jewish learning and activities.

### 🚀 CTeen

Dynamic programs for teenagers exploring Jewish identity, community service, and leadership.

## Ready to Get Started?

Help us build the programs your family wants to see! Let us know about your children's interests and we'll work together to create engaging Jewish programming for our community.
