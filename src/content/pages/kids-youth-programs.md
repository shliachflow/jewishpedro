## Youth & Family Programs

We are here to serve the Jewish community—from infants to seniors and everyone in between!

Community-Driven Programming: While we don't currently offer dedicated kids and youth programs, we'd love to make them happen based on your family's interests and needs.

If your children are interested in participating, let us know! Whether it's Mommy & Me, Hebrew School, CKids, CTeen, Young Jewish Professionals, or any other program, we're ready to bring it to life when we have families ready to join.

### Mommy & Me

Interactive early childhood programs for parents and toddlers to explore Jewish traditions together.

### Hebrew School

Jewish education covering the Hebrew language, prayers, and Jewish heritage.

### CKids

Jewish learning and activities for elementary school children.

### CTeen

Programs for teenagers on Jewish identity, community service, and leadership.

### Young Jewish Professionals (YJP)

Social, learning, and Shabbat gatherings for Jewish professionals, whenever there's a group ready to join.

## Ready to Get Started?

Help us build the programs your family wants to see! Tell us about your children's interests and we'll work with you to create them.
