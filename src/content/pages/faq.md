## What is Chabad?

A philosophy, movement, and organization with over 3,500 centers in 100 countries.

## Who can participate?

Our programs are open to all Jews, regardless of observance level. Most attendees are not Orthodox.

## How is the center funded?

Locally. We neither receive money from nor send money to Chabad headquarters in New York.

## Where are you located?

817 W 9th St, San Pedro, CA 90731, a commercial space we secured in December 2024.

## What programs do you offer?

Weekly classes (Tuesday evening on Zoom and Shabbat mornings), Shabbat dinners and meals, holiday celebrations, one-on-one learning with the Rabbi, women's gatherings, and services mainly on Shabbat and holidays. See [programs](/programs).

## Do you do hospital or prison visits?

The Rabbi visits Providence Hospital. Another Rabbi serves Terminal Island Correctional Facility.

## Where can I find kosher food?

Some kosher products are at Ralph's, with larger selections at kosher markets in Los Angeles. There are no kosher restaurants in San Pedro, but local caterers and meal delivery are available.

## Where are the nearest daily minyanim?

Congregations in Long Beach, Redondo Beach, Lomita, and Palos Verdes. Please confirm times with each one.

## Where is the nearest mikvah?

Lomita, Long Beach, and Redondo Beach. Contact us for details.

## What other services do you provide?

Mezuzah sales, Bar/Bat Mitzvah lessons, Kaddish and Yahrzeit help, Jewish burial assistance, tefillin wrapping, senior visits and frozen meals, and volunteer opportunities.

## What's not offered yet?

Childcare, an eruv, and a lending library (in development).

## How can I donate?

Use our [donation page](https://secure.givelively.org/donate/san-pedro-jewish-center).

## I need financial assistance.

Try Jewish Family Services of Los Angeles or Harbor Interfaith on 9th St.
