### What is Chabad?

Chabad is a philosophy, a movement, and an organization. It is one of the most dynamic forces in Jewish life today, with over **3,500 centers in 100 countries** dedicated to the welfare of the Jewish people worldwide.

[Back to top ↑](#)

### Are Chabad programs exclusively for Orthodox Jews?

Chabad is welcoming and non-judgmental, and our programs are open to all Jews. In fact, most people who attend Chabad programs are not Orthodox. Chabad avoids labels as they can create barriers between people. The teachings of Chabad are infused with the joy and warmth of Chassidic tradition, but no level of observance is required to participate.

[Back to top ↑](#)

### Is Chabad S. Pedro funded by its headquarters in New York?

No. Each Chabad center is independently funded by the community it serves. We do not receive financial support from Chabad headquarters in New York, nor do we send funds there. **All donations remain in our local community.**

### Is there kosher food in San Pedro?

**Grocery Stores:** Ralph's carries some kosher products. For a larger selection, Elat Market (Pico Blvd, Los Angeles) and other kosher markets in the LA area are accessible.

**Restaurants & Catering:** There are no kosher restaurants in San Pedro. However, there are numerous options in the greater Los Angeles area. We can refer you to local caterers for events.

**Deliveries:** Kosher meal delivery services are available to the San Pedro area. Contact us for current recommendations.
