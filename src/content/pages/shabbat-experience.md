**Saturday · Shabbat · Kiddush**

## A deep dive into Chassidic teachings

Weekly study of Chassidic texts on life's big questions: the nature of the soul, purpose, and serving G-d with joy.

## Uplifting prayer and song

Pray and sing popular Jewish melodies. Many find that prayer lowers stress and brings a sense of happiness and connection.

## Community connection

The kiddush lunch that follows is a chance to catch up with friends and meet new people.

## Schedule

- **9:45am** Chassidut class
- **10:15am** Prayer services and Torah reading
- **Afterward** Kiddush lunch

Open to experienced prayer-goers and newcomers alike. Location: 817 W 9th St, San Pedro, CA 90731. See [Synagogue Services](/synagogue-services) for more, or check [upcoming dates](/events).
