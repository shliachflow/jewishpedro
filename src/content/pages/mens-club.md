## Jewish Men's Club

Brotherhood, good food, cold beer, and meaningful connections every 4th Sunday of the month

## Welcome to the Brotherhood

Get together every 4th Sunday of the month to build friendships, community, have good food (and Beer!) and just have a good and meaningful time together with other Jewish Men in the area! Whether you're looking for camaraderie, spiritual growth, or just a great time with the guys, you'll find it here.

### Join the Brotherhood

Interested in joining our monthly gatherings? Contact Rabbi Yisroel to become part of our growing community of Jewish men who know how to have a good time while staying connected to their roots.
