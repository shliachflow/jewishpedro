The Men's Club is a monthly get-together for Jewish men in the area: friendship, good food, and meaningful conversation.

- **When:** usually the fourth Sunday of each month (dates may change)
- **Join:** contact Rabbi Yisroel
- **RSVP:** text [310-935-5236](tel:310-935-5236). You'll get the location, time, and menu, and you'll stay updated on future gatherings.

Past activities include game nights, BBQs, community service, and holiday celebrations. See the [events page](/events) for what's next.
