Rosh Hashanah: Sept 11–13 • Yom Kippur: Sept 20–21

Join us for a warm and uplifting Rosh Hashanah evening as we welcome the Jewish New Year with community, song, and traditional holiday foods. This year, the evening also ushers in Shabbat — a double joy of Shabbat and the New Year together. We'll share blessings for a sweet year ahead — think apples dipped in honey, round challah, and the joy of beginning anew.
