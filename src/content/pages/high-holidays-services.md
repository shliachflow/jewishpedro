ROSH HASHANA

### Rosh Hashana September 11–13

#### Rosh Hashanah Eve, Friday, September 11

- 6:45 P.M. — Candle-lighting & Evening Services
- 7:30 P.M. — Community Holiday Dinner

#### Rosh Hashanah Day 1 & Shabbat, Saturday, September 12

- 9:45 A.M. — Torah Class
- 10:15 A.M. — Morning Services, followed by a Kiddush Lunch

As Day 1 coincides with Shabbat this year, the shofar is not sounded — it is sounded on Day 2, along with Tashlich.

#### Rosh Hashanah Day 2, Sunday, September 13

- 10:00 A.M. — Morning Services
- 12:00 P.M. (approx.) — Shofar Sounding, followed by a Kiddush Lunch
- 5:15 P.M. — Tashlich @ Averill Park

YOM KIPPUR

### Yom Kippur September 20–21

#### Yom Kippur Eve, Sunday, September 20

- 6:34 P.M. — Candle-lighting | Fast Begins
- Kol Nidrei Evening Service

#### Yom Kippur Day, Monday, September 21

- 10:00 A.M. — Morning Services
- 12:00 P.M. (approx.) — Yizkor Memorial Service
- 5:30 P.M. — Mincha/Neila Closing Service
- 7:28 P.M. — Yom Kippur Ends, followed by Buffet Break-Fast
