## Jewish Holidays

Welcome to your guide to the beautiful cycle of Jewish holidays throughout the year. Each celebration brings its own unique traditions, meaning, and opportunities for spiritual growth and community connection.

From the reflection of the High Holy Days to the joy of festival celebrations, these sacred times offer moments to pause, celebrate, and deepen our connection to tradition, family, and community. Join us as we explore and experience these meaningful occasions together.

## Celebrate With Us

### Rosh Hashanah

The Jewish New Year, a time of reflection, renewal, and sweet beginnings as we welcome a fresh start with hope and intention.

[Learn More](https://www.chabad.org/holidays/default_cdo/jewish/Jewish-Holidays.htm)

### Yom Kippur

The Day of Atonement, our holiest day of spiritual cleansing, forgiveness, and reconnection with our highest purpose.

[Learn More](https://www.chabad.org/holidays/default_cdo/jewish/Jewish-Holidays.htm)

### Sukkot

The Festival of Booths, celebrating gratitude, harvest, and the divine protection that surrounds us throughout life's journey.

[Learn More](/sukkot-simchat-torah)

### Shemini Atzeret & Simchat Torah

Rejoicing with the Torah as we complete and begin anew the annual cycle of reading, celebrating our eternal connection to wisdom.

[Learn More](/sukkot-simchat-torah)

### Chanukah

The Festival of Lights, eight nights of celebrating miracles, dedication, and the triumph of light over darkness.

[Learn More](/chanukah)

### Tu B'Shvat

The New Year of the Trees, honoring our connection to nature and celebrating renewal, growth, and environmental stewardship.

[Learn More](https://www.chabad.org/tu-bshevat)

### Purim

A joyous celebration of hidden miracles and divine providence, filled with costumes, storytelling, and community festivities.

[Learn More](/purim-minisite)

### Passover (Pesach)

The Festival of Freedom, commemorating liberation from bondage and celebrating the eternal journey toward spiritual and personal freedom.

[Learn More](/passover-minisite)

### Lag BaOmer

A day of celebration during the counting period, honoring learning, joy, and the light of wisdom that brightens our lives.

[Learn More](/lag-baomer)

### Shavuot

The Festival of Weeks, celebrating the giving of the Torah and our eternal commitment to wisdom, learning, and spiritual growth.

[Learn More](https://www.chabad.org/shavuot)

Holidays begin at sundown on the evening before the first date listed.
