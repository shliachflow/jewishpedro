Every Jewish holiday is celebrated here, open to everyone, no tickets required. When a program is scheduled, details and RSVP appear on our [events page](/events). Until then, learn about each holiday on [Chabad.org](https://www.chabad.org/holidays/default_cdo/jewish/Jewish-Holidays.htm).

Holidays begin at sundown on the evening before the first date listed.
