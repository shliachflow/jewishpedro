Welcome to your guide to the cycle of Jewish holidays throughout the year. When a program is scheduled, details and registration appear on our [events page](/events). Until then, learn more about each holiday on [Chabad.org](https://www.chabad.org/holidays/default_cdo/jewish/Jewish-Holidays.htm).

| Holiday | Hebrew date |
|---|---|
| Rosh Hashanah | 1–2 Tishrei |
| Yom Kippur | 10 Tishrei |
| Sukkot | 15–21 Tishrei |
| Shemini Atzeret & Simchat Torah | 22–23 Tishrei |
| Chanukah | 25 Kislev – 2 Tevet |
| Tu B'Shvat | 15 Shevat |
| Purim | 14 Adar |
| Passover | 15–22 Nisan |
| Lag BaOmer | 18 Iyar |
| Shavuot | 6–7 Sivan |

For exact secular dates each year, see the [Jewish calendar](https://www.chabad.org/calendar/view/month_cdo/jewish/Jewish-Calendar.htm).
