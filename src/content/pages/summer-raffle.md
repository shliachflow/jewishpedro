> **Note for the center:** this page describes the summer raffle with a July 30 drawing. It was still on the old site, but the date has passed. Update or remove it.

**Win $10,000. Keep Jewish San Pedro growing.** Drawing July 30.

Every entry supports Chabad of San Pedro: classes, holiday celebrations, and one-on-one help.

## Prizes

- **Grand prize:** $10,000, raffled together with other Chabad centers
- **Second prize:** Apple AirPods 4
- **Local prize:** two challahs a week for four weeks (San Pedro / South Bay pickup)

## How to enter

Entries run from $36 for 1 entry up to $1,800 for 180 entries. [Enter through our secure donation form](https://secure.givelively.org/donate/san-pedro-jewish-center/summer-raffle). Zelle (info@jewishpedro.org) and Cash App ([$ChabadSPedro](https://cash.app/$ChabadSPedro)) were also accepted. Include your name and amount in the memo.

**No purchase necessary:** mail your name, address, phone, and email on a 3" × 5" card to Raffle – Chabad of San Pedro, 817 W 9th St, San Pedro, CA 90731.

You can also join the [Chai Society](https://jewishpedro.org/chaisociety), our monthly giving circle ($18/month or more, cancel anytime).

## Fine print

Raffle entries are not tax-deductible; a tax-deductible gift without entries is available through the form. Entrants must be 18 or older. The grand prize is raffled only in conjunction with other Chabad centers. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes and fees.
