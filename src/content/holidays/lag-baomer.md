## What it is

Lag BaOmer is the 33rd day of the Omer, the 49-day count between Passover and Shavuot. It falls on the 18th of Iyar. Tradition says a plague among the students of Rabbi Akiva stopped on this day, and it is also the anniversary of the passing of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, the author of the Zohar, the central text of Jewish mysticism.

## How it is celebrated

- **The mourning pauses.** The Omer is a period of partial mourning. On Lag BaOmer, the restrictions on weddings, music, and haircuts are lifted.
- **Bonfires.** Bonfires are lit in honor of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai's teachings, which brought light into the world.
- **Outings.** Children's parades, picnics, and outdoor games are a Chabad tradition. Bows and arrows are a custom for children.
- **Torah study.** It is a day for learning, especially the teachings of Chassidus and the Zohar.
