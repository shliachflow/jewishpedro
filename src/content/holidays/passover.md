## What it is

Passover begins on the 15th of Nissan and lasts eight days outside Israel. It commemorates the Exodus from Egypt and the birth of the Jewish people as a nation, when G-d freed us from slavery.

## How it is observed

- **Matzah.** We eat matzah, the unleavened bread, and avoid chametz (leavened grain products) for the whole holiday. Homes are cleaned of chametz beforehand, with a search on the night before Passover, and any remaining chametz is sold or burned.
- **The Seder.** On the first two nights, families gather for a ritual meal. We read the Haggadah, drink four cups of wine, and eat matzah, maror (bitter herbs), and charoset. The youngest asks the Four Questions. The afikoman, a piece of matzah, is hidden and then eaten at the end of the meal.
- **Fast of the Firstborn.** Firstborn males traditionally fast on the morning before the Seder, or attend a siyum (study celebration) instead.
- **The middle days.** These days are semi-festive. Foods stay kosher for Passover.
- **The last days.** The seventh day recalls the splitting of the Red Sea. The eighth day includes the Yizkor memorial prayers.
