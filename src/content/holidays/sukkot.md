## What it is

Sukkot is a seven-day festival starting on the 15th of Tishrei. It recalls the protection G-d gave the Jewish people in the desert after the Exodus, and it is known as "the season of our rejoicing."

## How it is celebrated

- **The sukkah.** We eat meals, and many people sleep, in a temporary hut covered with branches, open enough to see the stars.
- **Four species.** Each day, except Shabbat, we take the lulav (palm branch, with myrtle and willow) and the etrog (citron) and shake them in all directions during the morning prayers.
- **Chol Hamoed.** The middle days of the festival are semi-festive: work is limited and the holiday meals continue.
- **Hoshanah Rabbah.** The seventh day has a special prayer service in which we circle the synagogue seven times with the four species.
- **Guests.** There is a custom of welcoming guests into the sukkah, both friends and, symbolically, the seven great leaders of Jewish history.
