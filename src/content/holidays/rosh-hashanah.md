## What it is

Rosh Hashanah is the Jewish New Year, observed on the first two days of Tishrei. It marks the anniversary of creation and the day each year when we crown G-d as King. It opens the Ten Days of Repentance that lead to Yom Kippur.

## How it is celebrated

- **Shofar.** The ram's horn is sounded during the morning services on both days (not on Shabbat). Hearing it is the central mitzvah of the holiday.
- **Prayer.** Services are longer, with special prayers from the High Holiday prayer book.
- **Festive meals.** Candles are lit on both evenings. We dip apple in honey, eat round challah, and wish each other a sweet new year: "Shanah Tovah."
- **Tashlich.** On the first afternoon we go to a body of water and recite prayers, symbolically casting off the past year's mistakes.
- **No work.** As on Shabbat, work is set aside on both days.
