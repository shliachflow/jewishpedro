## What it is

The day after Sukkot ends, we celebrate two connected holidays on the 22nd and 23rd of Tishrei. Shemini Atzeret is a day of its own: we recite the Yizkor memorial prayers and begin saying the prayer for rain. Simchat Torah, the "Joy of the Torah," marks the end and the new beginning of the yearly Torah reading cycle.

## How it is celebrated

- **Hakafot.** In the evening and morning, we dance in circles around the synagogue with the Torah scrolls.
- **Everyone is called up.** On Simchat Torah, every adult is called to the Torah, and the children are called up together.
- **Finishing and starting again.** We read the last lines of Deuteronomy and immediately begin Genesis, to show that the Torah never ends.
- **Children's part.** Children carry flags and sweets and join the dancing.
- **Meals.** On Shemini Atzeret we still eat in the sukkah. Both days have a festive meal.
