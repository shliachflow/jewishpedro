## What it is

Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement, falls on the 10th of Tishrei. It is the holiest day of the year, a day of fasting, prayer, and forgiveness.

## How it is observed

- **Fast.** No food or drink from before sunset until nightfall the next day, about 25 hours. Anyone with a medical concern should ask a doctor and a rabbi.
- **Other restrictions.** No washing for pleasure, no leather shoes, no perfumes or lotions, and no marital relations. Work is set aside, as on Shabbat.
- **Services.** The day begins with Kol Nidre in the evening. The next day includes the morning service, the Yizkor memorial prayers, and Neilah, the closing service, which ends with a long shofar blast.
- **Before the fast.** A festive meal, candle lighting, and a memorial candle that burns through the day.
- **Forgiveness.** The ten days before Yom Kippur are a time to ask forgiveness from people we have wronged.
